Popular modular kitchen layouts

  • Linear (Straight): Best for narrow apartments; efficient workflow and minimal footprints.
  • L-shaped: Open-plan friendly; good for corner spaces and counters with additional storage.
  • U-shaped: Ideal for larger kitchens, provides continuous surfaces and back-of-house efficiency.
  • Island: Luxury & social kitchens — great for villas and high-end apartments in Bangalore.

Materials & finishes we recommend in Bangalore (durable + aesthetic)

Choose from engineered stone worktops (low maintenance), solid timber accents for warmth, and water-resistant laminate cabinetry for heavy-use zones. For high-humidity areas consider moisture-resistant MDF with quality laminates.

Countertops

  • Quartz (engineered stone) — durable, stain-resistant.
  • Granite — classic, heat resistant.
  • Solid surface (Corian) — seamless & repairable.
